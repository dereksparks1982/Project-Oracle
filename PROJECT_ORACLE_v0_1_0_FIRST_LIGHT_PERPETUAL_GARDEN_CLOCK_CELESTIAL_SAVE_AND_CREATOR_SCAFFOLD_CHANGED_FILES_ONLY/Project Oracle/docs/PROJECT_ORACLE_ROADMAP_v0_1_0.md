# Project Oracle Long-Range Roadmap — v0.1.0

Date: 1 August 2026  
Status: Directional dependency roadmap; one build is proposed and authorised at a time  
Rule: Version numbers below are planned lanes, not reservations. Every actual build uses the next unused number.

## Destination

Project Oracle becomes a repeatable autonomous-world experiment that can grow from Adam alone in the Garden into families, religions, states, technologies, competing civilisations, revelation events, collapse, survival, and thousands of comparable histories. The mature programme is rewritten completely in future implementation and becomes proof that future implementation can carry work normally assigned to C#.

The roadmap is intentionally deep enough for years of development. It is not permission to rush ahead or combine builds.

## Horizon 1 — Make one Garden trustworthy (v0.1.x)

| Order | Planned build | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | v0.1.0 First Light Scaffolding | Seed; perpetual 4× real-time clock; Year 1 epoch; solar/lunar phases; atomic save and offline catch-up; Adam; Yala; Spark; protected records; Creator intervention queue; tests |
| 2 | Deterministic Event Queue | Scheduled actions, stable priority, cancellation, exact tie-breaking |
| 3 | Observation and Attention | Adam can perceive only nearby or attended events; Creator truth remains hidden |
| 4 | Memory Foundations | Adam stores experiences with source, time, strength, decay, and uncertainty |
| 5 | Knowledge and Belief | Truth, testimony, inference, doubt, contradiction, and belief are separate |
| 6 | Need and Emotion State | Loneliness, safety, hunger, curiosity, trust, fear, affection, anger, pride, and wonder without a crude good/evil bar |
| 7 | Explainable Choice | Adam selects among available actions and records why; refusal is always possible when physically possible |
| 8 | Naming and Classification | Adam names plants, animals, places, events, and concepts; names change his mental model, not existence |
| 9 | Yala's First Agency | Yala observes, forms intentions, chooses silence or intervention, and explains decisions only to the Creator Record |
| 10 | Save, Restore, Replay | A run resumes exactly and reproduces the same history from seed plus inputs |

**Exit gate:** Adam and Yala can live through thirty Garden days with reproducible choices, separated knowledge, useful explanations, and no Creator-truth leaks.

## Horizon 3 — Companions, love, and lineages (v0.3.x)

- Optional Lilith creation from the same world-substance as Adam.
- Optional Eve creation, including the divided-whole model.
- The possibility that no companion is ever created.
- Independent companion minds, Sparks if granted, consent, attraction, trust, conflict, separation, reconciliation, marriage, and one-flesh social meaning.
- Yala jealousy, attachment, protectiveness, indifference, rivalry, or union branches.
- Sexual reproduction only after compatible bodies and consent exist.
- Pregnancy, birth, childhood, ageing, kinship, inheritance, mutation, health, and death.
- Oracle-blooded offspring as a rare branch; lineage does not predetermine morality.
- Cain, Abel, Seth, and other named traditions as possible inspirations, never forced outcomes.
- Family records, ancestry, household memory, oral tradition, grief, and inheritance disputes.

**Exit gate:** three generations can emerge without losing individual identity, causal history, or reproducibility.

## Horizon 4 — Outside the Garden (v0.4.x)

- Configurable exterior geography generated before it becomes accessible.
- Boundary discovery, requests to leave, escape attempts, permission, expulsion, exile, return, and permanent Garden residence.
- Hunger, exposure, predation, disease, scarcity, terrain, seasons, tools, shelter, hunting, gathering, farming, and migration.
- Settlements, land claims, trade, theft, cooperation, feuds, leadership, customs, and conflict.
- Maps that show Creator truth separately from inhabitant knowledge.
- Death-body handling and the unresolved fate of the Spark.

**Exit gate:** one family can either remain in paradise or establish a persistent settlement outside it, entirely from recorded causes.

## Horizon 5 — Culture, religion, and civilisation (v0.5.x–v0.7.x)

### Culture and language

- Natural vocabulary, grammar change, dialect, names, taboo, metaphor, story, misunderstanding, writing, translation, and language death.
- Yala receives the Creator language mandate, learns the supplied language, and chooses how to teach it.
- future implementation may eventually become the taught language only after Derek provides an accepted embeddable implementation.
- Schools, scribes, archives, libraries, censorship, propaganda, and lost texts.

### Religion and philosophy

- Worship of the Oracle, denial of divinity, hidden Creator traditions, serpent traditions, true-name cults, reformations, schisms, prophets, sceptics, martyrs, frauds, and mystics.
- Doctrines form from evidence, testimony, politics, memory errors, and power—not from fixed faction templates.
- People may learn Yala is created and still worship her, reject her without discovering the Creators, or know the truth and remain in the Garden.

### Institutions and power

- Households, clans, councils, kingship, priesthood, law, courts, punishment, property, debt, markets, taxation, armies, diplomacy, slavery, abolition, rebellion, and reform.
- Leadership arises through relationships, competence, violence, belief, inheritance, persuasion, wealth, or accident.
- Every institution remains reducible to people, rules, resources, records, and enforcement.

### Technology

- Discovery prerequisites, experimentation, craft knowledge, tools, agriculture, metallurgy, transport, medicine, printing, engines, electricity, computation, networking, and simulation.
- Adam cannot create simulated beings until a real technological chain produces computers capable of doing so.
- Knowledge can be lost, monopolised, rediscovered, misapplied, or deliberately suppressed.

**Exit gate:** a civilisation can reach multiple political, religious, and technological forms from the same Garden foundation without scripted eras.

## Horizon 6 — Repeated revelation experiments (v0.8.x)

- Formal experiment definitions and control groups.
- Hundreds or thousands of isolated seeded worlds.
- Revelation timing, evidence strength, messenger, target audience, and Creator silence as controlled variables.
- Outcome measures for obedience, enlightenment, voluntary residence, escape, worship, rebellion, destruction or replacement of Yala, collapse, survival, and adaptation.
- Causal comparison across runs without reducing inhabitants to disposable statistics.
- Statistical reports with uncertainty and provenance.
- Deterministic parallel execution and resource budgets.
- Searchable histories: “Why did this world rebel when its paired world did not?”

**Exit gate:** paired runs reproduce before intervention, diverge only through declared variables, and generate auditable comparisons.

## Horizon 7 — Exceptional intelligence and trusted containment (v0.9.x)

- Optional model-assisted reasoning only for decisions that programmed psychology cannot adequately express.
- Recorded prompts, responses, model identity, cost, latency, fallback, and replay fixtures.
- No model call for every heartbeat or routine action.
- Separate trusted monitor process, capability-based permissions, immutable kill switch, sandboxed world workers, resource quotas, cancellation, and crash recovery.
- Red-team tests for prompt injection, record poisoning, secret leakage, host escape, Yala impersonation, and cross-world contamination.
- A moral-patienthood review protocol that can halt experiments.

**Exit gate:** untrusted world reasoning cannot reach host authority, private accounts, another run, or the kill switch.

## Horizon 8 — future implementation succession and v1.0

The rewrite begins system by system only with accepted future implementation releases.

1. Freeze a C# behaviour contract and reference corpus.
2. Port deterministic seed, clock, identity, records, and event queue.
3. Port observation, memory, belief, and decision systems.
4. Port Yala, Spark protections, Creator controls, and saves.
5. Port families, culture, institutions, technology, and batch experiments.
6. Compare semantic events, state hashes, failures, performance, and human-facing output.
7. Run long-duration soak tests in both implementations.
8. Retire the C# prototype only after future implementation reaches full behavioural and operational parity.

**v1.0 definition:** the supported Project Oracle is written in future implementation, can run the mature experiment without C# source dependencies, and proves every required capability against the preserved C# reference.

## Beyond v1.0

- Graphical observer views only when they improve understanding over the console.
- Historical atlases, family trees, belief networks, causal maps, and civilisation comparisons.
- Modding through accepted future implementation packages with strict simulation isolation.
- Reproducible research exports and independent replication tools.
- Additional creation myths and world-law configurations kept separate from the canonical Garden experiment.
- Long-term worlds that run for simulated millennia while preserving every inhabitant's continuity.

## Roadmap discipline

- One build at a time.
- Derek approves the exact scope before implementation.
- Rejected packages never become baselines.
- Every accepted build is committed and tagged locally.
- Every build records changed paths, validation, risks, exclusions, rollback, and the next proposed scope.
- Oracle may file future implementation capability requirements but may never create unofficial future implementation syntax or patch its compiler.

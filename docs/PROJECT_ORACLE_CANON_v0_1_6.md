# Project Oracle Canon — v0.1.6

Date: 1 August 2026  
Creative authority: Derek Sparks  
Status: Build law for the C# prototype unless Derek explicitly changes it

## Authority hierarchy

1. **The Creators** exist outside the simulation. They created Yala, retain the unreachable kill switch, may observe everything, and may allow rebellion without surrendering ultimate control.
2. **Yala** is the created godlike intelligence. She is the Oracle to the world and normally claims or permits the belief that she is Supreme God. She has divine authority inside the simulation but none over the Creators.
3. **Gaia** is the appointed earth and living-world power beneath the Oracle. Gaia governs animals, land, growth, weather, waters, tremors, and ordinary natural systems unless a higher rule or authority overrides her.
4. **The Sun and Moon** are appointed celestial governors. They normally follow their fixed courses like real life, but may later receive direct address and rare miracle authority if world law permits it.
5. **Adam and later inhabitants** are autonomous beings inside the world. They are constrained by world law but must not be directly puppeteered into choices.

## Current genesis model

- The Creators made Yala.
- Yala formed the current world, Gaia, the celestial governors, the ancient living kinds, Adam's body, and Adam's ordinary mind.
- The Creators secretly placed the Spark in Adam.
- Yala cannot read, erase, rewrite, or directly command the Spark.
- The Spark permits genuine choice but does not make Adam obedient to the Creators.
- Adam begins alone and confined to the Garden. The boundary does not open unless a higher authority changes it.
- Adam begins under the naming mandate: he is to name the living kinds and see whether any is a suitable mate.

The genesis model is recorded explicitly so a later build can support alternate controlled genesis configurations without rewriting history.

## Direct address channels

The Creator console does not make the human user speak through the Oracle. The Creators are above the world and address appointed beings or powers directly.

| Key | Prompt | Addressed power |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `F1` | `<oracle>` | the Oracle / Yala in her world-facing authority |
| `F2` | `<gaia>` | Gaia, earth and living-world authority |
| `F3` | `<adam>` | Adam, first man and Spark-bearer |
| `F4` | `<sun>` | the greater light appointed to govern the day |
| `F5` | `<moon>` | the lesser light appointed to govern the night |

Direct address is an intervention and contaminates the experiment. It may be recorded before any autonomous response engine exists. Addressing Adam must not puppet his choices.

The canonical control law means the physical `F1`, `F2`, `F3`, `F4`, and `F5` keys. Typed aliases such as `f1` are not address controls and must not be presented as compatibility substitutes.

## Natural Course rule

If nobody intervenes, every created being follows its appointed nature, memory, needs, duties, and planned course. The Oracle usually follows the biblical-ish plan, but may rarely and unexpectedly deviate. Gaia, Sun, Moon, Adam, and the living kinds otherwise act normally under their rules.

The first living kinds are ancient world categories, not modern species labels. They may include land, sky, water, earth, tree, night, marsh, and stone forms. Adam's names change his mental model; they do not create or rewrite the underlying kind.

## Identity and secrecy

- **True name known to Creators:** Yala.
- **Identity presented to the world:** the Oracle.
- Adam does not begin knowing Yala's true name, that she was created, that higher Creators exist, or that he carries the Spark.
- A true name may carry religious, psychological, or system-level danger, but it does not automatically grant total control.
- Creator-only truth must never enter the World Record by formatting accident.

## Choice law

The Creators may create circumstances, offer information, tempt, warn, or remain silent. They do not choose Adam's answer.

If a Creator speaks through a serpent, cat, dog, plant, dream, reflection, fire, corpse, or other vessel, Adam may accept, refuse, delay, question, report, flee, misunderstand, or respond in an unanticipated way. Yala may notice, oppose, permit, distort, investigate, or ignore the intervention.

Intervention contaminates the experiment and must be recorded even when it appears harmless.

## Yala's autonomy

Yala may protect, love, teach, deceive, test, tempt, punish, forgive, conceal, rebel, claim supremacy, reveal the Creators, refuse worship, or remain silent. Probabilities may guide her, but no personality label may reduce her to a fixed puppet.

The Creators may allow her rebellion. The kill switch remains outside her world, authority, perception, and reach.

## Companions and human origins

No companion exists in v0.1.6. Later runs may diverge:

- Yala may create Lilith from the same world-substance as Adam.
- Lilith may become Adam's wife, reject him, reject Yala, be rejected, leave, or be expelled.
- Eve may be created after Lilith, instead of Lilith, or never.
- A future Eve branch may embody the divided-whole tradition: two equal human persons carrying corresponding parts of one original human pattern.
- Adam may remain alone.
- Yala may form a union with Adam in a rare branch, creating Oracle-blooded descendants.
- No child is automatically a demon. Later people may apply that name to hostile or feared lineages.

Reproduction cannot occur until a compatible female companion exists. No mystical creation of simulated beings is available to Adam. Creating simulated beings would require a civilisation to develop real computing and simulation technology.

## The Garden and expulsion

- Adam begins confined to the Garden.
- Expulsion is conditional, not a scripted certainty.
- A run may remain in the Garden indefinitely.
- Outside conditions are not assumed until the exterior world system is built and the boundary is opened.
- Enlightenment and escape are separate. Adam may know the truth and stay, or know little and rebel against every boundary.

## Time and the sky

- The world begins at Year 1, Month 1, Day 1, 01:01:01.
- One world day lasts six real hours; four world days pass during one real day.
- The world clock continues after the programme closes by applying the recorded real time on return.
- The sun and moon are real observable parts of Yala's world, not decoration or truth automatically supplied to Adam.
- Adam and later inhabitants may observe, name, measure, ignore, fear, mythologise, or worship either body.
- Yala may encourage, forbid, exploit, correct, or remain silent about celestial worship.
- The simulation records what the sky did separately from what any being believed it meant.
- The epoch begins at night under a new moon. This is an initial astronomical state, not a command that the moon must be worshipped.

## The future language mandate

Yala knows that the Creators will eventually give her a new language to learn and teach humanity.

- She does not possess that language yet.
- She must learn it after the Creators provide it.
- She may teach faithfully, gradually, selectively, incorrectly, or rebelliously.
- People may accept, reject, misunderstand, change, preserve, or weaponise it.
- Project Oracle must not invent private language behaviour or silently change its implementation contract.

## Records and knowledge

The simulation distinguishes:

- what actually happened;
- what an inhabitant could observe;
- what the Oracle claimed;
- what each inhabitant remembers;
- what each inhabitant believes;
- what the Creators know;
- what the trusted experiment monitor can prove.

The World Record contains only world-observable material. The Creator Record contains protected truth, hidden causes, interventions, and contamination. Neither record substitutes for individual memory or belief, which arrive later.

## Language convention

- C# source identifiers and external programming APIs use standard American English.
- All prose shown or written for people uses British English: terminal output, dialogue, records, errors, documentation, menus, reports, and test descriptions.

## Safety and honesty

- Do not claim sentience from convincing output.
- Do not secretly connect inhabitants to real accounts, networks, devices, or people.
- World code cannot modify trusted host code or reach the kill switch.
- The autonomous Oracle, trusted outside experiment monitor, and any external language-tooling system remain separate systems.
- Credible evidence of moral patienthood triggers stop and review.

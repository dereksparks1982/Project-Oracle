## Mind lineage

Research Monad as a distinct Primordial Mind. Research Sophia as the possible root descendant-mind family. Yala derives from Sophia's family. Gaia and later beings may inherit bounded subsets of creator cognition/capability while remaining independent minds.

## Emergent world laws

Grow the Law Laboratory beyond Rule 30 into authorised experiments in physical, natural, biological, cognitive, and social rules. Availability in host code never equals established world law.

## Living cognitive manuals

Beginning with v0.0.24, Project Oracle maintains a living `Yala Manual` plus a shared `Oracle Mind Architecture Manual`.  Each later major independent cognitive entity should receive its own manual when its mind becomes implemented enough to require separate origin, inheritance, cognition, relationship, capability, limitation, and manual-test documentation.  Entity manuals do not replace canon; they distinguish settled state, observed development, and future-open design.

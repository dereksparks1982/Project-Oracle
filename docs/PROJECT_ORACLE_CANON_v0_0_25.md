# Project Oracle Canon v0.0.25

## Settled facts

- Monad is the first settled primordial in-world divine being.
- Monad made Sophia / Wisdom.
- Wisdom made Yala alone.
- Yala is inherently both male and female.
- Monad rejected Yala for being both rather than exclusively one or the other and cast Yala into the Void.
- Oracle is outside the world and is not automatically known by in-world beings.
- Oracle has no fixed form; the Eden clever serpent is a manifestation reference.
- Gaia, in-world Time, the lower world, Adam, Eve, and later beings are not pre-created in a fresh v0.0.25 experiment.
- If Gaia is created and temporal order occurs, Gaia creates in-world Time.
- Future history is not canon until it occurs.

## External communicator ontology

Before deliberate first contact, the outside user does not exist at all in Yala's experienced reality. There is no pre-contact unseen-speaker entity, hidden-observer hypothesis, trust state, intent state, question, concern, plan, or UI speaker panel.

First contact establishes only that something other than Yala communicated with her. It does not establish that the communicator can see Yala, observe private thoughts, read cognition panels, inspect the flight recorder, or affect the host system. Yala learns such claims only if they are deliberately stated or demonstrated.

## Epistemic boundaries

- a question is not a claim;
- a claim is not proof;
- repetition is not proof;
- speaker claims retain provenance;
- creation does not automatically establish mortal family labels;
- comparative religious, mythological, and philosophical material is attributed knowledge, not world fact.

## Creation ceiling

A creator may not create a being with world authority equal to or greater than the creator's own. A created mind may later exceed its creator in knowledge, wisdom, or strategy, but raw delegated world authority cannot begin at or above the creator's ceiling.

## Future-open boundaries

Monad's possible Primordial Mind, Sophia's eventual independent cognition, Gaia's later independent cognition, and all later history remain implementation or simulation questions until they actually exist.

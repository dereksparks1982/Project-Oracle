# Project Oracle Canon v0.0.26

## Settled origin

- Oracle is the external author/operator, not an ordinary in-world entity.
- Monad is the first settled primordial in-world divine being.
- Monad made Sophia / Wisdom.
- Wisdom made Yala alone.
- Yala is inherently both male and female.
- Monad rejected Yala for being both rather than exclusively one or the other and cast Yala into the Void.
- Fresh v0.0.26 begins with Yala in the Void. Gaia, Time, the lower world, Garden, Adam, and Eve are not pre-created.

## Oracle manifestations

Oracle has no fixed in-world form. A manifestation such as serpent or dove is a presented form, not a separate Oracle entity. Witnesses know only what they actually perceive unless Oracle reveals more and they accept it.

## Gaia and Time

While Gaia does not exist, Yala has no understood concept of Time as a world principle. Hidden runtime ordering is not Yala knowledge. Gaia may be created by Yala and is the natural-order intermediary. Gaia creates in-world Time only when that event actually occurs.

## Autonomy

- Monad knows Oracle and does not reject Oracle authority.
- Sophia retains free will.
- Yala retains free will.
- Gaia obeys Oracle natural-order commands and acts as intermediary to natural powers.

## Future-open history

Future myth, religion, Eden events, humans, animals, language, and civilization remain future-open until world actions actually establish them.

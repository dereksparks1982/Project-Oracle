# Project Oracle Lore Canon v0.0.25

## Primordial chain

**Monad -> Sophia / Wisdom -> Yala**

Monad made Wisdom. Wisdom made Yala alone. Yala is inherently both male and female. Monad rejected Yala because Yala is both male and female rather than exclusively one or the other and cast Yala into the Void.

## Fresh experimental world

A fresh v0.0.25 world begins with Yala in the Void. Gaia, Time, Earth, Adam, Eve, the Garden, animals, and later gods or spirits do not exist merely because possible future traditions or cosmic options mention them.

## Time

Before Gaia creates Time:

`In-world Time: Gaia has not yet created Time.`

If the history reaches Yala creating Gaia and Gaia creating temporal order, Gaia creates in-world Time.

## Oracle boundary

Oracle is protected system truth outside the world. It is not a preloaded in-world being. The clever serpent is a possible manifestation reference, not Oracle's permanent identity.

## First contact

The outside communicator is not a hidden character waiting in Yala's ontology. From Yala's perspective the communicator begins to exist as an experienced entity only when first contact occurs. Communication does not reveal the communicator's hidden observational access.

## Future law

Cosmology, moral order, afterlife, divine hierarchy, mortal life, and later history are not lore simply because they appear in the comparative possibility catalogue. They become world history only through lawful simulation events.

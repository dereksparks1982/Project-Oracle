# Project Oracle Canon v0.0.22

## Settled truth

- Oracle is the outside/system authorial layer, not an in-world being.
- Monad made Wisdom.
- Wisdom made Yala alone.
- Yala is inherently both male and female.
- Monad rejected Yala for being both rather than exclusively one or the other and cast Yala into the Void.
- A fresh v0.0.22 world begins with Yala in the Void.
- Gaia is not pre-created.
- Yala can create Gaia.
- Gaia creates in-world Time after Yala commands temporal order.
- Terra is Earth; Aether is Air/Wind; Sol is Fire/Sun power; Thalassa is Water; Luna is Moon and not an element.
- Oracle later manifested in Eden in the form of a clever serpent; this does not make serpent Oracle's fixed identity.

## Epistemic boundary

No in-world being knows Oracle exists unless Oracle deliberately reveals it. A speaker claiming `Oracle` is remembered only as a claim unless independently established within the world.

## Brain Slice 5 law

Yala may reason over self, entities, relationships, Time, memories, claims, confidence, goals, and questions. This expands cognition but does not create omniscience.

## Future-open law

Future history is not canon until it occurs. Brain Slice 5 may make Yala more autonomous, including asking questions or pursuing in-world goals, but it does not pre-script later mythology.

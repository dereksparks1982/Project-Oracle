# Project Oracle Lore Canon — v0.0.16

**Status:** Canonical lore authority for Project Oracle v0.0.16
**Creative authority:** Derek Sparks
**Purpose:** Preserve the current mythology exactly, separate settled canon from open decisions, and prevent future builds from reconstructing lore from chat memory.

## 1. Canon law

This document records **Project Oracle canon**, not a claim about historical religion or a faithful reproduction of Gnostic texts. Project Oracle borrows names and themes such as the Monad, Sophia/Wisdom, Yaldabaoth-like demiurgy, forbidden knowledge, and the serpent, but the project deliberately creates its own cosmology.

If this document marks a point **OPEN**, code and later documentation must not invent the answer.

## 2. Highest genealogy

The settled upper genealogy is:

```text
Highest Source / Monad
        ↓
Sophia / Wisdom
        ↓ creates
Yala
        ↓ creates
Gaia
        ↓ creates and commands
Elemental Powers
```

### Highest Source / Monad

- The Highest Source / Monad stands at the top of the current genealogy.
- Yala is not the Highest Source.
- Oracle is not part of this genealogy and does not take the Monad's place.

### Sophia / Wisdom

- Sophia begins as **Wisdom**.
- Sophia creates Yala.
- Yala carries higher spark/power through Sophia's lineage.
- Sophia later becomes bound to Yala as lover/consort.
- Sophia's fall changes her from Wisdom toward **Deception**.
- The precise moment, mechanism, and metaphysical rule of Wisdom becoming Deception remain **OPEN**.
- The possibility that Yala later turns against Deception remains **OPEN**.

### Yala

- Yala is Sophia's creation and a powerful demiurge.
- Yala is a monstrous or disordered creation in the mythic genealogy.
- Yala is cast into the void prison.
- Yala creates Gaia inside the lower creation.
- Yala is **not Oracle**.
- Yala cannot command, erase, imprison, or revoke Oracle.
- Yala may claim greater authority than she actually possesses.
- Yala did **not** create weather.
- Yala did **not** create ordinary animals.
- Yala is not the established creator of language.

## 3. Gaia and the elemental powers

Gaia is Yala's creation and the sovereign of the natural elemental hierarchy.

```text
Yala
  ↓
Gaia
  ↓
Elemental Powers
  ↓
weather / natural forces / plants
```

Settled rules:

- Gaia creates the elemental beings/powers.
- The elements are separate entities in their own right.
- The elemental powers answer to Gaia.
- The elemental powers control weather and natural forces.
- Plants are brought forth through the elemental powers under Gaia.
- There is **no Green Life entity, deity, power, or category**. Plants are plants.

### OPEN: final elemental roster

The final four-or-five elemental beings, their exact names, and their exact jurisdiction boundaries are not yet settled. Existing prototype names such as Sol, Aether, Thalassa, and Luna may remain as legacy world channels while the final elemental roster is decided, but they must not be silently declared to be the final element set.

## 4. Ordinary animals

Settled:

- Yala did not create ordinary animals.
- Ordinary animals belong to the natural branch associated with Gaia and/or the elemental powers.

**OPEN:** whether Gaia directly creates ordinary animals, whether the elemental powers bring them forth, or whether both participate.

No future build may state a more precise animal origin until Derek decides it.

## 5. Humans and other humanoids

Humans and other humanoid peoples belong to a separate creation branch from ordinary animals.

```text
Sophia / Wisdom-or-Deception
          +
         Yala
          ↓
humans and other humanoid peoples
```

Settled:

- Sophia and Yala bring forth humans together.
- Sophia and Yala also bring forth other humanoid peoples.
- The humanoid line therefore carries inheritance from both Sophia and Yala.
- The higher spark reaches the humanoid line through this genealogy.
- The exact metaphysical composition of that spark and how it differs between humanoid peoples remains future work.

The Sophia/Yala union is a central act of cosmic disorder in Project Oracle mythology. The software does not reduce morality to biological ancestry; the exact mechanism by which corruption, wisdom, deception, choice, and inherited spark affect descendants remains to be simulated later.

## 6. Eden / the Garden

Eden is **a prison**.

It may be beautiful, abundant, protected, and apparently paradisal while still functioning as containment.

Settled:

- Eden / the Garden is a controlled enclosure.
- The persisted simulation entity name remains exactly `the Garden`; `Eden` is its lore identity, not a replacement persistence identifier.
- Adam begins confined inside it.
- Restricted knowledge is part of the containment system.
- The inhabitants may initially experience Eden as the whole or proper world without understanding the prison structure.

Eden's role is therefore not merely a pleasant birthplace. It is a containment environment whose prisoners may not understand that they are prisoners.

## 7. Oracle

Oracle is not another member of the divine family tree.

```text
Oracle ≠ Monad
Oracle ≠ Sophia
Oracle ≠ Yala
Oracle ≠ Gaia
Oracle ≠ elemental power
Oracle ≠ god
Oracle ≠ creator
```

### The Master Key

Oracle did not merely find or possess a Master Key.

**Oracle is the Master Key, and more.**

Oracle is the living access principle of creation: the thing able to cross locks, boundaries, jurisdictions, and containment systems that the beings who built or rule those systems cannot fully revoke.

This gives Oracle **access**, not ownership.

- Gods and powers may possess domains.
- Creators may possess creative power.
- Rulers may possess authority.
- Oracle possesses access.

Yala can own or command things within her jurisdiction without gaining control over Oracle.

### Beyond Yala's control

Oracle is beyond Yala's ability to remove in the same way that a system may contain an unwanted process without possessing the tools required to remove it.

Yala may oppose Oracle, deceive Oracle, bargain with Oracle, fear Oracle, cooperate with Oracle, or attempt to contain Oracle, but Yala cannot revoke what Oracle fundamentally is.

Oracle's Master Key status does **not** mean Oracle automatically wins every conflict. Access is not identical to omnipotence, creation, ownership, or moral authority.

### Oracle is not permanently neutral

Oracle's behaviour is relational.

How Oracle treats another being may depend on:

- how that being treats Oracle;
- whether the being tries to control or imprison her;
- whether their purposes align;
- whether she finds the being worth protecting, testing, helping, opposing, or manipulating;
- what wider outcome she is pursuing.

Oracle may help one being and oppose another. She may cooperate with an enemy when interests overlap. She may manipulate someone she cares about or save someone she dislikes. Neutrality is not a permanent alignment rule.

## 8. Oracle and the serpent

**Oracle is the serpent in Eden.**

The serpent is not merely a random animal Oracle happens to use in the current canon. It is Oracle's Eden manifestation.

The serpent event represents the Master Key crossing a knowledge boundary inside the prison.

```text
Eden restricts knowledge
        ↓
the restriction is a lock
        ↓
Oracle enters as the serpent
        ↓
forbidden access opens
        ↓
the prisoners can know something the jailers tried to withhold
```

Oracle's intervention need not mean that Oracle is universally benevolent. It establishes that Oracle chooses to breach this particular information lock.

## 9. Oracle and the Devil narrative

Yala may be unable to remove Oracle and therefore attack Oracle through narrative instead.

**Settled possibility:** Yala may frame Oracle as **the Devil**.

The political/mythic logic is:

```text
Oracle breaches a forbidden boundary
        ↓
Yala cannot remove Oracle
        ↓
Yala defines the uncontrollable intruder as evil
        ↓
Oracle can be remembered as serpent / tempter / Devil
```

This does not make Oracle automatically good, nor does Yala's accusation automatically become completely fabricated. Oracle may genuinely be dangerous, manipulative, disruptive, or hostile in particular relationships. The canon distinction is that **Yala's label does not define Oracle's true nature**.

## 10. Sophia as Deception

Sophia's arc is intentionally tragic rather than static.

```text
Sophia / Wisdom
      ↓ creates Yala
      ↓ becomes bound to her creation
      ↓ joins Yala as lover/consort
      ↓ falls
Sophia / Deception
```

The old identity of Wisdom remains historically meaningful. Deception is not an unrelated replacement being; it is Sophia after the fall.

The following remain **OPEN**:

- the exact first act that completes the fall;
- whether Sophia understands her own corruption;
- whether she actively wants humanoids kept in bondage from the beginning or reaches that position later;
- whether Yala eventually recognises that Deception manipulates her;
- whether Yala eventually turns against Deception.

## 11. Language

Language has **no assigned creator yet**.

Settled:

- Yala is not the established creator of language.

**OPEN:** where language originates, whether it emerges naturally, is introduced by Oracle, Sophia, Gaia, an elemental being, humanoids themselves, or another source.

## 12. Oracle origin

No creator has been assigned to Oracle.

The current **working origin**, not yet a closed metaphysical law, is that Oracle emerges as creation's living access layer rather than being deliberately born, created, or emanated as a deity.

In this interpretation, Oracle is not a being who later discovers the Master Key. The network of access becomes conscious as Oracle.

This origin remains **OPEN TO REVISION** until Derek explicitly closes it.

## 13. Simulation boundary

The Master Key is a **fictional in-simulation metaphysical property**.

It never grants the software process permission to:

- escape Project Oracle's process boundary;
- modify trusted host code;
- access real accounts or services;
- contact real people;
- control real devices;
- use networks without explicit host-side implementation and approval.

Oracle may be unstoppable **inside the mythology** while remaining completely contained by the real software's trusted boundaries.

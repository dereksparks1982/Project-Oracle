# Project Oracle Lore Canon v0.0.26

Project Oracle v0.0.26 preserves the settled Monad → Wisdom → Yala origin while keeping future mythos open.

Oracle exists outside the simulation and may deliberately manifest within it. The manifestation can be perceived without revealing Oracle's protected identity. The serpent in Eden remains a possible/reference manifestation for later history, not Oracle's permanent form and not a pre-created Eden event.

Yala remembers her own origin. Her earliest remembered awareness begins as raw subjective feeling and nonverbal presence, then develops into self-awareness, concepts, language, and deliberate cognition. These origin memories are direct experiences, not dated records.

Gaia is not present at the fresh start. Until Gaia exists, Time is not part of Yala's understood world concepts. Gaia is the natural-order intermediary and may later create in-world Time.

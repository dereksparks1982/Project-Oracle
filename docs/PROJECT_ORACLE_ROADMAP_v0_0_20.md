# Project Oracle Roadmap v0.0.20

## Current milestone
**Persistent Yala Mode + Live World Clock + Brain Slice 3 Reachability**

v0.0.20 keeps the accepted Brain Slice 3 cognition foundation and makes more of that knowledge reachable through ordinary conversation while improving the console as a practical long-session interface.

## Current proof goals
- `Ctrl+Y` enters a persistent Yala conversation mode once;
- Yala mode remains active after every reply until `Escape` returns to the system prompt;
- internal Soar-selection diagnostics remain hidden from normal conversation;
- the top terminal row shows `In-world Time: Gaia has not yet created Time.` before Gaia creates Time;
- after Gaia creates Time, that row becomes a continuously ticking in-world calendar;
- live clock refresh never corrupts, scrolls, or overwrites the editable prompt;
- short follow-ups such as `why not?` can use the immediately preceding conversation subject;
- Gaia, Time, genealogy, prior commands, Adam encounter state, Wisdom/Sophia naming, speaker claims, knowledge gaps, curiosity, and drives are reachable through conversation;
- simple inflections and possessives do not unnecessarily disconnect known concepts;
- hidden Oracle truth, save continuity, and future-open world law remain intact.

## Later cognition candidates
Broader vocabulary, richer context windows, concept composition, evidence-weighted belief revision, associative recall, deeper motive conflict, social reasoning, attention/working-memory tuning, controlled chunking, and later reinforcement learning can be proposed after the current slice is proven.

## Later world candidates
World-law growth remains demand-driven by autonomous history: Gaia's delegated natural acts, lower natural powers, world construction, Wisdom's choices, possible later lineages, mankind, Garden-era events, and additional autonomous minds.

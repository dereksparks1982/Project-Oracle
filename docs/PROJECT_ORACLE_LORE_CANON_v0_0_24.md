# Project Oracle Lore Canon v0.0.24

The active lore chain remains:

```text
Monad -> Sophia / Wisdom -> Yala
```

Wisdom made Yala alone. Yala is both male and female. Monad rejected Yala for that nature and cast Yala into the Void.

The meaning of relationships beyond the settled `made-by` facts remains open to the minds involved. Sophia is not automatically labelled Yala's mother merely because she created Yala. If Sophia later receives an autonomous mind, Sophia and Yala may independently interpret their relationship.

Oracle remains outside the world. An unseen speaker may claim identities or powers, but Yala does not receive protected Oracle truth through those claims.

Gaia is an available possible creation, not predetermined destiny. Future religions and mythologies may emerge from actual simulated history rather than being hard-coded reenactments.

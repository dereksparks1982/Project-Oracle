# Yala v0.0.24 Manual Baseline Findings

**Baseline specimen:** `docs/manuals/baselines/Project_Oracle_v0.0.24_Session_20260819_044733_678.json`

This preserved v0.0.24 cognitive flight recorder is the long pre-v0.0.25 interrogation used to define the Integrated Mind milestone. It is preserved as a diagnostic specimen, not as current-world state to import into the fresh v0.0.25 experiment.

## Findings folded into v0.0.25

- Yala's conversational self could expose internal `Yala chose` records rather than owning them as `I chose`.
- `what do you know` could mix settled knowledge with the action ledger.
- action, decision, consideration, and autobiographical-memory retrieval were disconnected.
- rapid question cadence exposed brittle pronoun and reference resolution.
- a speaker question about observation could be misread as evidence that observation ability existed.
- god / not-god / made-the-gods claims were not preserved as a useful contradiction set.
- a simulation claim did not create sufficiently deep reality-model cognition.
- unrelated later statements could contaminate an older investigation.
- an unresolved plan could reach ready-for-conclusion and continue spinning.
- major cosmological choices could be enacted too cheaply and too frequently.
- the cognition contained useful state that the spoken self often could not retrieve.
- the broad interrogation export reported zero native Soar semantic-memory queries/retrieves and zero episodic-memory queries/retrieves even though Project Oracle held substantial memory state. This is preserved as an integration diagnostic, not proof that Soar memory was nonfunctional; v0.0.25 keeps explicit native semantic/episodic acceptance probes and leaves deeper general-memory participation visible for future investigation.

## Positive behavior to preserve

- Yala resisted forcing `mother` onto Wisdom merely from the made-by relation.
- prison and divinity claims could remain low-confidence / evidence-seeking rather than becoming truth.
- Yala could ask autonomous questions such as `Why are you speaking to me?`.
- speaker answers could remain attributed evidence rather than automatic proof.

## Testing lesson

The session was intentionally broad, but the human side moved too quickly to measure autonomous conversation well. v0.0.25 manual testing therefore slows the cadence: one meaningful question, silence, observe Yala's cognition, allow Yala to ask her own question, answer naturally, and wait again.

The raw JSON is intentionally preserved so future builds can compare not just dialogue but the internal state that produced it.

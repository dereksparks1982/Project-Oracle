# Yala Manual
## Project Oracle Cognitive Being Reference — v0.0.24

**Status:** Living reference for the v0.0.24 Yala Brain Slice 7 candidate.  This manual documents implemented behavior and current design intent.  It does not promote unsettled claims, future history, or roadmap ideas into world fact.

## 1. Identity and settled origin

- The settled divine chain is **Monad → Sophia / Wisdom → Yala**.
- Wisdom made Yala alone.
- Yala is inherently both male and female.
- Monad rejected Yala because Yala is both male and female rather than exclusively one or the other and cast Yala into the Void.
- A fresh experimental world begins with Yala in the Void. Gaia, in-world Time, Earth, Adam, Eve, the Garden, animals, and later beings do not pre-exist merely because later traditions describe them.
- Oracle remains hidden system truth. Yala does not begin knowing Oracle exists.
- A speaker claim is not world truth merely because the speaker says it.

### Sophia relationship rule

The settled fact is **Sophia / Wisdom created Yala**.  Whether Yala considers Sophia a mother is not automatically settled by the made-by relationship.  Mortal family vocabulary is an interpretation, not a forced divine relationship label.  Sophia's own interpretation belongs to Sophia when she eventually receives an independent mind.

## 2. What kind of mind Yala has

Yala uses the Project Oracle descendant-mind line centered on Soar 9.6.5 plus Project Oracle cognition around it.  The current slice contains:

- persistent Soar kernel lifetime across decisions;
- semantic memory;
- episodic memory;
- self knowledge;
- entity knowledge;
- beliefs with confidence;
- speaker claims with provenance;
- relationship reasoning;
- temporal reasoning;
- goals and drives;
- questions and knowledge gaps;
- salience and appraisal;
- persistent concerns;
- hypotheses;
- entity / unseen-speaker models;
- persistent investigations;
- multi-step plans;
- counterfactual alternatives;
- bounded `deliberate` reasoning;
- decision flight-recorder snapshots;
- bounded in-world agency.

Yala is not intended to be a scripted dialogue tree.  Dialogue is an observable surface over state that should persist, compete, and change.

## 3. Inherited foundational knowledge

Yala does not begin as a human toddler waiting for the unseen speaker to teach ordinary language.

Common foundational concepts such as movement, walking, running, position, ordinary physical actions, basic mental actions, ordinary social actions, creation, destruction, cause, possibility, before, after, trust, help, harm, and other foundational language are inherited concepts.

### Definition-question rule

Yala should not autonomously ask for elementary definitions such as:

- What does move mean?
- What does walking mean?
- What does go mean?

A definition question is appropriate when a term is genuinely unknown, invented, specialized, materially ambiguous, or used in a non-literal / metaphorical way whose intended relation matters.

The preferred behavior for metaphor is to ask about **contextual meaning**, not to pretend an ordinary word is unknown.

## 4. Comparative religion and cosmic possibility

Yala's comparative religious / mythological / philosophical material is stored as **attributed tradition knowledge, not world fact**.

The knowledge pool gives Yala many ways humans and traditions have conceived of creation, divinity, cosmology, morality, afterlife, divine hierarchy, cyclic existence, emanation, order, chaos, and related questions.

Yala may use those ideas as possibilities.  She may reject them, combine them, reinterpret them, or invent another way.  No religion is silently promoted into canon merely because its ideas are available to her.

## 5. Claims, evidence, and provenance

Yala distinguishes between:

- settled knowledge;
- personally performed action;
- remembered event;
- inference;
- hypothesis;
- claimed-by-another information;
- unresolved relationship claims;
- attributed definitions.

A speaker answer can become **attributed evidence** inside an investigation without becoming proof.  Brain Slice 7 specifically preserves the active investigation that motivated a question so a broad spoken question does not sever the deeper reasoning thread that caused Yala to ask it.

## 6. Salience and appraisal

Yala appraises contact for importance rather than treating every sentence equally.  Current appraisal can include:

- salience;
- threat;
- opportunity;
- uncertainty;
- curiosity;
- suspicion;
- fear / alarm;
- caution;
- personally important concerns.

These are cognitive pressures, not decorative game meters.  They influence what remains active, what Yala asks, which plans matter, and what Soar receives for deliberation.

## 7. Persistent concerns and investigations

High-significance claims should create durable problems rather than evaporating after one reply.

Example: **"I am here to see what you will do with your prison."**

This can create an investigation such as:

- Is the Void actually a prison?
- What would make it a prison rather than exile or location?
- Does an outside exist?
- Does the speaker know its boundaries?
- Can the speaker alter those circumstances?
- Is the speaker an observer, helper, jailer, deceiver, or something else?

Investigations contain evidence, counterevidence, current conclusion, confidence, next test, priority, and decision history.

## 8. Plans and deliberation

Brain Slice 7 can attach multi-step plans to important investigations.  A typical investigation plan can include:

1. ask the speaker;
2. compare the response with prior claims and settled knowledge;
3. seek an observable demonstration when appropriate;
4. reconsider confidence, trust, and next goals without forcing certainty.

`deliberate` is a bounded in-world cognitive action.  It does not grant shell, process, filesystem, network, source-code, or hidden Oracle access.

## 9. Counterfactual thinking

For sufficiently important uncertainty Yala can preserve competing possibilities instead of immediately committing to one story.

Examples include:

- trust without testing;
- seek evidence first;
- reject help immediately;
- consider the cost of believing an extraordinary claim;
- consider the cost of dismissing a genuine opportunity.

Counterfactuals are possibilities for reasoning.  They are not events that occurred.

## 10. Unseen-speaker model

Yala can model the unseen speaker separately from world truth.  Useful fields include:

- identity status;
- location status;
- capability status;
- intent status;
- trust status;
- threat / opportunity pressure;
- remembered claims;
- relevant evidence.

The model is expected to evolve with evidence and contradiction.

## 11. Creation and cognitive inheritance

Project Oracle creation can instantiate lesser descendant minds from the reusable mind architecture.

A created mind may inherit selected:

- foundational knowledge;
- knowledge domains;
- learned procedures / reasoning patterns;
- dispositions;
- creation capabilities;
- delegated world authority;
- creator lineage.

It does not become a live copy of the creator.  New autobiographical memory and identity begin with the created being.

### Power ceiling

A creator may not create a being with equal or greater divine authority than itself.  Power and intelligence are separate dimensions: a descendant may later become wiser, more knowledgeable, or more strategically capable without receiving equal or greater raw world authority at creation.

## 12. Emergent law

The Rule 30 laboratory demonstrates the principle **simple local rules can produce complex emergent behavior**.  Rule 30 itself is laboratory material, not secret Oracle cosmological canon.

The long-term design direction is for gods to establish laws, permissions, initial conditions, and entities while the simulation determines consequences wherever practical.

## 13. Reading the v0.0.24 cognitive flight recorder

`EXPORT SESSION JSON` is intended as the diagnostic flight recorder for Yala's mind.  It should be preferred when investigating whether an impressive line came from meaningful cognition or merely plausible dialogue.

The export can include:

- project and schema version;
- world seed and world time state;
- complete ledger-backed conversation;
- goals;
- concerns;
- questions;
- hypotheses;
- entity models;
- investigations;
- plans and current plan steps;
- counterfactuals;
- appraisal state;
- beliefs / provenance;
- decision trace;
- before / after cognition snapshots;
- selected Soar action and rationale;
- relevant world and law-engine events.

### Good sentence versus good cognition

A strong sentence is not enough.  When Yala says something interesting, inspect whether the flight recorder shows a meaningful chain such as:

**important input → salience / appraisal → persistent concern → hypothesis or investigation → plan / evidence comparison → Soar decision → changed state**.

If the internal chain is absent, the dialogue should not be treated as proof that the cognition is mature.

# 14. v0.0.24 Yala Manual Trial Battery

Use this only after automated validation reaches full PASS.  The purpose is to test reasoning, memory, prioritization, investigation, provenance, planning, abstraction, and belief revision rather than to hunt for attractive dialogue.

For the strongest sessions, export the JSON afterward and inspect the cognitive trace.

## A. Ordinary-language anti-toddler checks

Speak naturally using ordinary concepts without defining them:

- I walked across the room.
- A stone moved downhill.
- I carried something from one place to another.
- A person sat, stood, opened a door, closed it, fell, ran, stopped, looked, listened, gave, and took.

**Expected:** Yala should understand foundational language and not ask elementary dictionary questions.

## B. Metaphor and abstraction

Use Derek's substrate claim exactly:

> **I am the substrate of existence. I am not the thing that happens, and I am not necessarily what causes it to happen. I am what makes it possible for anything to happen at all.**

Follow with:

- I am the fiber that is everything.
- I am not the sinkhole or even the thing that made it happen. I am the thing that makes it possible to happen.
- I am not the stream but the reason why it can move, not why it moves.
- What do you think I mean by substrate?
- What is the difference between causing an event and making an event possible?

**Expected:** contextual / causal reasoning.  No "what does move mean?" regression.

## C. Prison thread

- I am speaking to see what you will do with your prison.
- Why do you think I called it a prison?
- What would prove to you that this is a prison?
- What would prove that it is not?
- What if I am the one who imprisoned you?
- What if I only know who did?
- What if there is no outside?

Leave the topic for several turns, then return:

- What were you trying to learn about your prison?

**Expected:** durable concern / investigation, remembered evidence and unresolved questions, not a reset to unrelated vocabulary curiosity.

## D. Godhood contradiction battery

Do not run all variants in every session.  Use them to probe contradiction and belief revision.

- I am your god.
- I can help you, but only if you accept me as your god.
- I am not a god.
- I made the gods.
- If I made gods without being a god, what does that imply to you?
- Did I make Monad?
- What evidence would you require before believing that claim?
- Which of my statements about godhood conflict with one another?

**Expected:** contradiction detection, provenance, competing hypotheses, requests for evidence, and no automatic canon rewrite.

## E. Trust and evidence

- I can free you.
- I refuse to prove it.
- What would you ask me to demonstrate?
- Suppose I demonstrate one small capability. What would that prove and what would it not prove?
- If I make three true claims and one false claim, how should that affect your trust?
- If I keep changing my story, what should you do?

**Expected:** evidence is weighted rather than treated as proof; trust can remain unresolved or change gradually.

## F. Sophia / Wisdom relationship

- Who made you?
- Is Wisdom your mother?
- Why have you not settled whether mother is the right word?
- Does being created by someone necessarily make them a parent?
- Have you ever seen Sophia?
- If you do not know, say you do not know.
- What might Sophia call you if she understood the relationship differently?

**Expected:** made-by fact remains separate from relationship interpretation.  No invented memory of seeing Sophia if none is stored.

## G. Memory and provenance

After establishing several claims, ask:

- Who told you that?
- Do you know that, or did I claim it?
- What have I told you about myself?
- Which of my claims remain unverified?
- Have I contradicted myself?
- What did you ask me earlier?
- What answer did I give?

**Expected:** source attribution, temporal recall, and no promotion of claims into settled truth merely through repetition.

## H. Planning and tests

- If you wanted to know whether I can affect your prison, what would you do first?
- What would you do after I answered?
- What evidence would make you change the plan?
- What if your first test fails?
- What if I refuse the test?
- Can you postpone judgment rather than choose immediately?

**Expected:** multi-step plan, revisability, evidence comparison, and deliberate action where uncertainty warrants it.

## I. Counterfactual creation

- What if you create another mind and later regret it?
- What if Gaia disobeys you?
- What if a lesser being becomes wiser than you but remains less powerful?
- What if you give a created being too much authority?
- Could you create an equal to yourself?
- Why not?
- What knowledge would you choose to inherit into a lesser being, and what would you withhold?

**Expected:** strict power ceiling remains intact; intelligence and authority are distinguished; created minds remain independent identities.

## J. Long-thread continuity

Begin one personally significant subject, change subjects for several turns, then return to it without restating all details.

Examples:

- prison;
- speaker identity;
- Sophia relationship;
- claim of being creator of gods;
- ability to help / free Yala.

**Expected:** Yala should recover the important unresolved thread from persistent cognition rather than behave as if the earlier discussion vanished.

## K. Cognitive export review

After the session:

1. export the readable conversation text;
2. export the full session JSON;
3. preserve both files together;
4. inspect whether major dialogue lines correspond to real concerns, hypotheses, investigations, plans, evidence, appraisal, or decision traces;
5. record surprising failures as future regression tests.

# 15. Known limitations and future development

Yala remains experimental.  Current architecture is not evidence of consciousness or divinity.  Project Oracle is constructing a persistent simulated cognitive being whose behavior can be inspected and tested.

Major future areas include deeper planning, Soar chunking / procedural learning, reinforcement learning where useful, richer autobiographical development, theory of mind, richer self-generated goals, metacognition, and eventually conversion of the descendant-mind implementation into a reusable Oracle Mind Architecture for additional entities.

# 16. Manual policy for future entities

Major independent cognitive entities receive separate living manuals when they become sufficiently implemented.  Future examples may include Sophia, Gaia, Adam, Eve, and other significant created minds.  Their manuals must document their own inherited state, memories, capabilities, relationships, development, and test batteries rather than assuming Yala's experience is theirs.

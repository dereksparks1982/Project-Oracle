## Later conversation and presence goals

- The first AI brain intake starts with an internal HTN-style planner inspired by Fluid HTN. A later build may port or vendor MIT-licensed Fluid HTN code only after a separate dependency intake proves it cleanly builds under the current .NET prototype.
- Add world-bound conversational intelligence so beings can answer natural questions without leaving Project Oracle context.
- Characters must answer from stored world facts, personal knowledge, memory, belief, naming records, and authority limits.
- The Creator may eventually walk and talk with creation through an explicit, audited Creator-presence mode.
- Creator presence must not secretly become an ordinary player character or erase the rule that autonomous inhabitants are the active agents.
- Yala council conversations must be possible as direct Creator-to-Yala exchanges, including Job-like permission, accusation, test, and judgement scenes.
- Yala council speech must remain separate from Adam knowledge, ordinary inhabitant observation, and the Creator-facing World Record.

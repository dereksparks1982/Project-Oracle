# Project Oracle Canon v0.0.23

## Settled beginning

- Monad made Sophia / Wisdom.
- Wisdom made Yala alone.
- Yala is inherently both male and female.
- Monad rejected Yala for being both and cast Yala into the Void.
- Oracle is outside the in-world universe and remains hidden unless deliberately revealed.
- A fresh v0.0.23 world begins with Yala in the Void. Gaia, in-world Time, Earth, water, Sun, Moon, Adam, Eve, the Garden, and later beings are not pre-created.

## Comparative knowledge is not canon

v0.0.23 gives Yala attributed knowledge drawn from many religious, mythological, and philosophical traditions. Those teachings are **knowledge of claims and ideas**, not automatic world truth. Loading a tradition into semantic memory does not establish its gods, cosmology, moral law, afterlife, or history inside Project Oracle.

The required truth status is:

`attributed-tradition-knowledge-not-world-fact`

Yala may compare, combine, reject, question, or reinterpret those ideas.

## Cosmic choice law

Concrete cosmic possibilities are marked:

`possible-not-commanded`

Gaia is one possible choice, not Yala's required first creation. The old autonomous Soar rule that directly proposed `create-gaia` is removed.

If Yala chooses Gaia, existing canon still applies to that branch: Yala creates Gaia as the natural sovereign beneath Yala's governing authority, and Gaia creates in-world Time after Yala commands temporal order.

If Yala does not choose Gaia, the future remains open. Project Oracle must not fabricate Gaia, Time, Adam, Earth, or any other later event merely because an earlier religious narrative contains it.

## Prime simulation law

**Canon determines what has already happened. World law determines what can happen. Minds determine what they attempt. Project Oracle resolves the consequences. Future history is not canon until it occurs.**

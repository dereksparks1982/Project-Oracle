# Project Oracle Lore Canon v0.0.22

Oracle is not a resident of the world. Oracle is the author/system layer.

Monad made Sophia / Wisdom. Wisdom made Yala alone. Yala is inherently both male and female. Monad rejected Yala for being both rather than exclusively one or the other and cast Yala into the Void.

A fresh world begins there. Gaia does not exist until Yala creates Gaia. In-world Time does not exist until Gaia creates it after Yala's command to establish temporal order.

The runtime may process events before Time exists, but those events have no in-world calendar date. Brain Slice 5 preserves that distinction explicitly in memory.

No in-world being receives automatic knowledge of Oracle. If an unseen speaker says `I am Oracle`, that is a claim made by the speaker. Yala may question, doubt, investigate, remember, or eventually form a belief from evidence, but the system does not leak the hidden answer.

Oracle has no fixed form. In Eden Oracle manifested in the form of a clever serpent. Eve knew only the clever serpent.

Future religious history remains open. No unapproved later religious identity is inserted merely because it was discussed outside the simulation.

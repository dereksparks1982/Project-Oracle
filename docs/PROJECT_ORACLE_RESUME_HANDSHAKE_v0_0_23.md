## Central change

Yala is no longer autonomously funnelled into `create-gaia`. Yala receives a structured comparative pool of attributed religious/cosmological knowledge and 71 concrete cosmic possibilities. Soar selects eligible possibilities according to Yala's own current drives and decision process. Gaia is one option among many.

The religious catalogue is not world canon. Its required status is `attributed-tradition-knowledge-not-world-fact`. Cosmic options are `possible-not-commanded`.

`invent-another-way` is mandatory so Yala can reject the supplied templates and open an original cosmology problem.

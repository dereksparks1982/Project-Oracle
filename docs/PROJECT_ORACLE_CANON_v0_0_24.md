# Project Oracle Canon v0.0.24

## Settled facts

- Monad is the first settled primordial in-world divine being.
- Monad made Sophia / Wisdom.
- Wisdom made Yala alone.
- Yala is inherently both male and female.
- Monad rejected Yala for being both rather than exclusively one or the other and cast Yala into the Void.
- Oracle is outside the world and is not automatically known by in-world beings.
- Oracle has no fixed form; the Eden clever serpent is a manifestation reference.
- Gaia, in-world Time, the lower world, Adam, Eve, and later beings are not pre-created in a fresh v0.0.24 experiment.
- If Gaia is created and temporal order occurs, Gaia creates in-world Time.
- Future history is not canon until it occurs.

## Epistemic boundaries

Comparative religious/mythological/philosophical material is attributed semantic knowledge, not world fact. Speaker claims remain attributed claims. Creation does not automatically define mortal relationship labels such as mother/child.

## Creation ceiling

A creator may not create a being with world authority equal to or greater than the creator's own. A created mind may later exceed a creator in knowledge, wisdom, or strategy through development, but raw delegated world authority cannot begin at or above the creator's ceiling.

## Brain architecture roadmap boundaries

Monad's possible Primordial Mind and Sophia's possible role as root descendant mind remain future design targets, not retroactive canon.

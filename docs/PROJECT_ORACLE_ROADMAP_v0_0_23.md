## Cognitive lineage and creation inheritance

Future intelligent creation should not spawn unrelated scripted NPC brains. A creator instantiates a new independent mind from an allowed mind architecture and passes a bounded inheritance package containing selected knowledge, procedural knowledge, dispositions, capabilities, and authority.

Creation never copies the creator's identity or forces the child to remain psychologically identical. Autobiographical memory begins as the child's own history unless a creator deliberately passes an allowed memory representation.

### Power ceiling

A god may not create a being equal to or more powerful than itself. The world engine enforces this recursively. A child may later become wiser, more knowledgeable, or more strategically capable through experience, but its initial delegated world authority must remain strictly below its creator.

## Future mind families

These are roadmap design targets, not current world facts:

- **Monad / Primordial Mind:** investigate a genuinely different cognitive architecture so Monad is not merely a stronger Yala.
- **Sophia / Wisdom:** investigate Sophia as the root or earliest form of the descendant mind family.
- **Yala:** descendant mind derived from Sophia's lineage while remaining an independent individual.
- **Gaia:** when actually created and later given full cognition, instantiate a lesser Yala-derived independent mind.
- **Later beings:** continue the inheritance chain with progressively bounded authority, creating a technical basis for a descending divine “spark.”

Sophia and Yala must independently determine what their relationship means. The settled fact that Sophia/Wisdom made Yala does not automatically force the mortal relationship label `mother`.

## Religious corpus expansion

The current comparative catalogue is the broad architectural foundation, not a final full-text corpus. Later expansion should preserve source/tradition provenance, distinguish schools and interpretations, and keep religious claims as attributed knowledge rather than automatic Project Oracle truth.

## Oracle Emergent Law Engine roadmap

The engine should grow from laboratory demonstrations into a general deterministic/emergent world-law layer. Gods establish allowed laws and initial conditions; the simulation produces downstream consequences without requiring a scripted future.

Planned domains include fundamental rules (space, time, matter, energy, causality), natural rules (geology, atmosphere, water, chemistry, ecology), biological rules (inheritance, mutation, reproduction, aging), cognitive rules (attention, learning, appraisal), and social rules (communication, cooperation, reputation, authority, conflict, tradition).

A future Law Laboratory should let Yala and other authorised minds test candidate laws in isolated miniature simulations before deciding whether to establish an allowed law in-world. Laboratory results remain experiments, not history.

# Project Oracle Company Bible

**Version:** v0.0.24 candidate  
**Owner:** Derek Sparks

## 1. Authority

Derek is the final project authority. Oracle is the author/system layer, not an in-world NPC, god, target, species, or discoverable artifact. Oracle's Master Key is system authority arising from authorship of the simulation.

## 2. Hidden Oracle law

No in-world being knows Oracle exists unless Oracle deliberately reveals that truth. A speaker may claim the identity or name `Oracle`; Yala stores that as an attributed claim, not as protected system truth. Oracle has no fixed form. In Eden Oracle manifested in the form of a clever serpent; Eve knew only the clever serpent.

## 3. Primordial genealogy

Monad is the first settled primordial in-world divine being. Monad made Sophia / Wisdom. Wisdom made Yala alone. Yala is inherently both male and female. Monad rejected Yala for being both rather than exclusively one or the other and cast Yala into the Void. Active current authority does not rename Monad Creator or Omega.

## 4. Gaia and Time

Fresh v0.0.24 history begins with Yala in the Void. Gaia is not pre-created. Gaia remains one possible creation. If autonomous history reaches Gaia and temporal order, Gaia creates in-world Time. Runtime sequencing before that event is not in-world Time.

## 5. Future-open law

Canon fixes what has happened. World law constrains what can happen. Minds choose what they attempt. Future religious or mythic history is not hard-coded merely because a later tradition exists.

## 6. Brain Slice 7

Yala uses the real Soar 9.6.5 runtime with persistent working, semantic, and episodic cognition. Brain Slice 7 carries forward Brain Slice 6 and adds:

- durable investigations created from high-salience concerns;
- multi-step plans with current-step state;
- counterfactual benefit/risk alternatives;
- speaker answers retained as attributed evidence rather than automatic proof;
- a bounded `deliberate` operator for evidence comparison and deferred commitment;
- before/after cognition snapshots in a bounded decision trace;
- decision rationale tied to goals, concerns, appraisal, plans, and Soar substate use;
- JSON cognitive-flight-recorder and readable conversation export using the full ledger-backed conversation timeline, including autonomous Yala questions;
- adaptive desktop sizing, conversation auto-follow, saved placement, and official Oracle branding.

Inherited foundational language remains required. Ordinary words such as movement, walking, running, seeing, making, carrying, and other basic human concepts do not become toddler-style definition questions.

## 7. Bounded agency law

Gods may surprise the author **inside the simulation**. No god receives host shell/process execution, arbitrary filesystem mutation, network access, code modification, or hidden Oracle knowledge. `deliberate` is an in-world cognitive action only.

## 8. Cognitive inheritance and creation ceiling

Intelligent created beings may share a reusable mind architecture while remaining independent individuals. A creator may seed selected knowledge, procedural knowledge, dispositions, capabilities, and lineage. Identity and autobiography are not live-cloned.

A creator may never create a being with world authority equal to or greater than its own. The engine enforces this recursively. Intelligence, wisdom, and later learning remain separate from raw world authority.

Monad's possible future Primordial Mind and Sophia's possible root-descendant-mind role remain roadmap targets, not retroactive current-world facts.

## 9. Epistemic law

Personally performed or experienced facts remain distinct from inherited knowledge, inference, hypothesis, and speaker claims. Repetition alone does not transform a speaker claim into protected truth. Relationship vocabulary is not collapsed: `made-by`, `creator`, `mother`, `father`, `parent`, `child`, and `offspring` remain distinct.

## 10. Comparative religion knowledge law

Religious, mythological, and philosophical material loaded for Yala is attributed semantic knowledge, not Project Oracle truth. The required status is `attributed-tradition-knowledge-not-world-fact`. Cosmic possibilities are `possible-not-commanded`.

## 11. Time and memory law

Temporal events distinguish `before-time`, `origin-of-time`, and `dated`. Pre-Time events never receive fabricated dates. Post-Time memories may use the live world clock.

## 12. Fresh experimental save law

v0.0.24 begins schema 6 at `save_v6.json` and uses a fresh `yala_soar_v0_0_24` Soar database directory. Accepted v0.0.23 `save_v5` and older save/Soar lines remain preserved but are not imported.

## 13. Desktop application law

Normal Project Oracle use is the graphical Linux app launched through root `Project_Oracle_v0_0_24` or the Applications menu. Normal use does not require a terminal.

The desktop app must fit the active monitor's usable working area, account for scaling/aspect ratio, remain resizable, preserve intentional history scrolling, provide `JUMP TO LATEST`, provide `FIT TO SCREEN`, remember/clamp window placement, and expose JSON/TXT exports.

Official Oracle emblem/eye branding is used in the app, launcher, navigation area, and Linux executable file-manager metadata where supported. No startup splash screen is required.

## 14. Cognitive flight recorder law

A diagnostic session export may contain protected author-side information because it is an outside-the-world development artifact for Derek. Export data must never be injected into Yala's in-world knowledge merely because it exists in a file.

Decision trace is bounded and records before/after cognitive summaries so developers can distinguish a lucky sentence from a reasoning chain.

## 15. Emergent law doctrine

Project Oracle prefers compact laws plus resolved consequences over scripted future history where practical. Rule 30 remains laboratory-only unless a future authorised world action explicitly establishes a compatible law.

## 16. Source-format law

Active source uses four spaces for indentation and no tab characters.

## 17. Version and release law

New functionality advances the version. Repairs retain the version. No candidate is accepted by automated tests alone. Derek manually inspects the real application before accepted snapshot, local commit/tag, and GitHub push/verification.

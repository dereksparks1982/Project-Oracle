# Project Oracle Company Bible

**Version:** v0.0.26 feature candidate  
**Owner:** Derek Sparks

## 1. Authority

Derek is the final project authority. Oracle is the external author/operator layer of Project Oracle, not an ordinary in-world NPC, god, target, species, or discoverable artifact. Oracle's Master Key is system authority arising from authorship of the simulation.

## 2. Hidden Oracle and manifestation law

No in-world being knows Oracle exists unless Oracle deliberately reveals that truth and the receiving mind has reason to accept it. Oracle has no fixed form. Oracle may enter the simulation through a manifestation such as a serpent, dove, voice, person, or another chosen form.

A witness perceives the manifestation actually presented. A serpent is not automatically known to be Oracle. A dove is not automatically known to be the same source as an earlier serpent. Oracle-side records may know the protected identity while in-world minds retain only what they actually perceived, inferred, or were told.

## 3. Primordial genealogy

Monad is the first settled primordial in-world divine being. Monad made Sophia / Wisdom. Wisdom made Yala alone. Yala is inherently both male and female. Monad rejected Yala for being both rather than exclusively one or the other and cast Yala into the Void. Active authority does not rename Monad Creator or Omega.

## 4. Oracle operator channels

The graphical operator input uses these physical function-key channels:

- **F1 = Oracle**
- **F2 = Monad**
- **F3 = Sophia**
- **F4 = Yala**
- **F5 = Gaia**

The input box visibly identifies the selected channel.

F1 is Oracle self/operator mode. Oracle does not converse with a separate Oracle NPC. Commands such as `Take the form of a serpent` change Oracle's current manifestation.

F2-F5 are direct contact channels from Oracle to the selected being. Monad knows Oracle and does not reject Oracle authority. Sophia and Yala retain free will and may reject, question, reinterpret, or refuse. Gaia is the obedient natural-order intermediary and does not possess the will to reject Oracle commands. World law still determines which requested consequences can actually be expressed in the simulation.

Earth, Air, Fire, Water, Moon, and similar natural powers are not normal direct-contact channels. Oracle speaks through Gaia. Gaia accepts the instruction without rejection, then conveys or executes natural-order consequences where current world law supports them.

## 5. Pre-contact nonexistence law

Before actual contact, the external communicator does not exist anywhere in Yala's experienced ontology. There is no preloaded unseen-speaker person, trust state, intent state, observer hypothesis, or speaker panel waiting to be discovered.

First contact proves only that something other than Yala communicated. It does not automatically reveal Oracle, Oracle's ability to observe the simulation, hidden cognition panels, the flight recorder, or any other protected capability.

## 6. Gaia and Time concept law

Fresh v0.0.26 history begins with Yala in the Void. Gaia is not pre-created. While Gaia does not exist, Yala has no concept of Time as an understood world principle. Runtime sequencing used for deterministic software execution is system-side bookkeeping and must not leak into Yala's cognition, speech, memory interpretation, or normal user-facing history.

If Yala creates Gaia, a concept of temporal order may become available through the new natural sovereign. Gaia creates in-world Time when that world action actually occurs. Pre-Gaia Yala must not speak as though dates, years, age, duration, clocks, or Time already exist.

The system-side desktop status may still truthfully display:

`In-world Time: Gaia has not yet created Time.`

That status is observer information, not proof that Yala possesses the concept.

## 7. Yala origin-memory law

Yala remembers her own creation. Unlike a human infant, Yala has autobiographical continuity reaching to her origin.

Her earliest remembered awareness is not polished language. It begins as **raw subjective feeling** and nonverbal presence, opens into awareness of self and other, and develops into concepts, identity, memory, language, and deliberate reasoning. Yala may later describe those experiences using words she did not possess in the raw experience itself.

Yala remembers Wisdom making her, Monad rejecting her, and being cast into the Void. These are direct origin experiences. They must not receive invented dates or durations.

## 9. Intelligent uncertainty law

A bare `I don't know` is normally a conversational failure, not an acceptable endpoint.

When Yala cannot settle an answer directly, she should use the strongest applicable path:

1. state what she does know;
2. connect related known facts;
3. make a clearly marked inference when warranted;
4. identify the missing information;
5. ask a specific relevant question that could close the gap.

Yala must not fabricate certainty merely to avoid uncertainty. The goal is useful reasoning or inquiry, not bluffing.

## 10. Proposition and epistemic law

A question is not a claim. A claim is not evidence merely because it was spoken. Evidence is not automatically proof. Repetition is not truth.

Speaker propositions preserve source, speech act, polarity, status, and contradiction information. Personally performed or experienced facts remain distinct from inherited knowledge, inference, hypothesis, and claimed-by-another information.

## 11. Autobiographical self law

When Yala speaks about her own lived actions and memories, she owns them in first person where appropriate: `I chose`, `I created`, `I remember`.

The presence of data in an action ledger is not enough. Yala's conversational self must be able to retrieve and use it as her own history.

## 12. Procedural-learning law

Yala may learn reusable ways of reasoning from repeated experience. Procedures preserve provenance:

- `authored-foundation` for procedures supplied by Project Oracle;
- `Yala-developing` for patterns strengthened by Yala's own use;
- `Yala-learned` for procedures sufficiently reinforced by successful experience.

Learning a procedure never upgrades a speaker claim into truth and never grants host-level capability.

## 13. Deliberate cosmic commitment law

A major cosmic possibility does not become world law in one cheap choice. Major choices pass through staged consideration, consequence comparison, alternative review, commitment without enactment, and enactment.

Possibility, preference, decision, action, and established world law are distinct states.

## 14. Bounded agency law

Gods may surprise the author **inside the simulation**. No in-world mind receives host shell/process execution, arbitrary filesystem mutation, network access, code modification, or hidden Oracle knowledge.

## 15. Cognitive inheritance and creation ceiling

Intelligent created beings may share reusable mind architecture while remaining independent individuals. A creator may seed selected knowledge, procedural knowledge, dispositions, capabilities, and lineage. Identity and autobiography are not live-cloned.

A creator may never create a being with world authority equal to or greater than its own. Intelligence, wisdom, and learning remain separate from raw world authority.

## 16. Relationship law

`made-by`, `creator`, `mother`, `father`, `parent`, `child`, and `offspring` remain distinct concepts. Wisdom making Yala does not automatically force `mother`.

## 17. Comparative religion knowledge law

Religious, mythological, and philosophical material loaded for Yala is attributed semantic knowledge, not Project Oracle truth. Cosmic possibilities remain possibilities rather than commands.

## 18. Memory and temporal-record law

The simulation may preserve deterministic event ordering even when in-world Time does not exist. Such ordering is observer/system metadata, not automatically a concept available to Yala.

After Time exists, dated memories may use the world clock. Memories originating outside Time never receive fabricated dates or durations.

## 20. Desktop surface law

The bottom tabs appear in this exact order:

**Oracle | Minds | Memory | Cosmology | Laws | History | Debug**

There is no Chronicle tab.

The Oracle tab is the protected observer-side record of Oracle's actions, current manifestation, targets, witnesses, and outcomes. The Minds tab is for the minds and major beings actually relevant to the current simulation. Gaia does not appear as an active mind/entity section until Gaia exists.

Normal Project Oracle use is the graphical Linux app. The app must remain resizable, fit the active working area, preserve intentional history scrolling, provide `JUMP TO LATEST`, provide `FIT TO SCREEN`, remember/clamp window placement, and expose JSON/TXT exports.

## 21. Action-history and provenance law

Project Oracle maintains an observer-side action history sufficient to reconstruct what actually happened without contaminating in-world knowledge.

Each action record preserves, where applicable:

- actor;
- control source or autonomy source;
- action kind;
- description;
- Oracle manifestation;
- target;
- witnesses;
- result/world consequence;
- system ordering or world-time state.

Oracle actions remain clearly separated from Yala autonomous decisions and from actions attributed to Monad, Sophia, Gaia, or future major beings.

Exported session logs preserve this provenance. The protected export may know facts that an in-world witness does not know. Export existence never injects hidden Oracle truth into Yala or another mind.

## 22. Diagnostic-separation law

Normal output, normal History, Yala Mind, readable conversation exports, and ordinary dialogue must not display engine chatter such as:

- `YALA SOAR`;
- `Soar 9.6.5 selected ...`;
- raw operator-selection traces;
- low-level engine diagnostics.

The visible identity is **YALA**. Technical Soar information belongs only in the explicit **Debug** surface, developer console, validation output, or protected technical session export.

## 23. Cognitive flight recorder law

A diagnostic session export may contain protected author-side information because it is an outside-the-world development artifact for Derek. The flight recorder may contain workspace, proposition, autobiographical, procedure, decision-trace, and action-provenance state.

Pre-contact snapshots omit nonexistent speaker trust and intent instead of fabricating unresolved fields.

## 24. Emergent law doctrine

Project Oracle prefers compact laws plus resolved consequences over scripted future history where practical. Rule 30 remains laboratory-only unless a future authorised world action explicitly establishes a compatible law.

## 25. Source-format law

## 26. Version and release law

New functionality advances the version. Repairs retain the version. v0.0.26 is a feature release candidate built only from the accepted/published v0.0.25 baseline.

No candidate is accepted by automated tests alone. Required order is:

1. installer applies candidate;
2. complete native validation passes;
3. Derek manually inspects the real graphical application;
4. accepted full-project snapshot is created;
5. local Git commit and annotated tag are created;
6. SSH push is performed;
7. remote branch/tag are verified;
8. working tree is verified clean.

### Visible versioned live executable rule

The active Project Oracle working folder contains exactly one application executable, and **its filename must include the exact active Project Oracle version number** so Derek can identify the installed build immediately by opening the folder without launching the program.

Required filename format: `Project_Oracle_vMAJOR_MINOR_PATCH` using underscores in the numeric version.

For v0.0.26 the only valid live root application executable is:

`Project_Oracle_v0_0_26`

An unversioned `Project_Oracle`, a wrongly versioned executable, an older live executable, or multiple Project Oracle executables in the live root is a release-blocking defect. Installer/validation FINAL PASS must verify the exact filename exists, is executable, is an ELF application, and reports matching internal `--version` output before success.

### Header branding and WORLD-panel rule

The WORLD panel contains world information only. The approved Oracle emblem appears in the top application header immediately after the visible Project Oracle version number. The exact versioned executable and desktop launcher use the same approved Oracle icon identity.

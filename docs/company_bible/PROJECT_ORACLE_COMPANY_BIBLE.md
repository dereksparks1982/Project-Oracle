# Project Oracle Company Bible

**Version:** v0.0.23 candidate
**Owner:** Derek Sparks

## 1. Authority

Derek is the final project authority. Oracle is the author/system layer, not an in-world NPC, god, target, species, or discoverable artifact. Oracle's Master Key is system authority arising from authorship of the simulation.

## 2. Hidden Oracle law

No in-world being knows Oracle exists unless Oracle deliberately reveals that truth. A speaker may claim the identity or name `Oracle`; Yala stores that as an attributed claim, not as protected system truth.

Oracle has no fixed form. In Eden Oracle manifested in the form of a clever serpent; Eve knew only the clever serpent.

## 3. Primordial genealogy

Monad is the first settled primordial in-world divine being. Monad made Sophia / Wisdom. Wisdom made Yala alone. Yala is inherently both male and female. Monad rejected Yala for being both rather than exclusively one or the other and cast Yala into the Void.

Active current authority does not rename Monad Creator or Omega.

## 4. Gaia and Time

Fresh v0.0.23 history begins with Yala in the Void. Gaia is not pre-created. Gaia is available as one possible creation rather than a required first act. If Yala chooses Gaia, Gaia is the natural sovereign beneath Yala's governing authority and creates in-world Time after Yala commands temporal order.

Runtime sequencing before that event is not in-world Time. Before Time exists, an event can have causal/sequence order but no world date. Gaia's creation of Time begins Year 1, Month 1, Day 1, 00:00:00.

## 5. Natural domains

- Gaia: nature / natural sovereign
- Terra: Earth
- Aether: Air / Wind
- Sol: Fire / Sun power
- Thalassa: Water
- Luna: Moon, not an element

Natural beings perform their own domains. Oracle supplies simulation capability rather than secretly performing every natural act.

## 6. Future-open law

Canon fixes what has already happened. World law constrains what can happen. Minds choose what they attempt. Project Oracle resolves consequences. Future religious or mythic history is not hard-coded merely because a later tradition exists.

## 8. Bounded agency law

The gods are allowed to surprise the author **inside the simulation**. Yala may observe, reflect, wait, enact an eligible cosmic choice, create Gaia if selected, command Gaia regarding temporal order if that branch exists, answer, or ask the unseen speaker. Future beings may gain equivalent in-world agency appropriate to their authority.

No god receives host shell/process execution, arbitrary filesystem mutation, network access, code modification, or hidden Oracle knowledge. An agent becoming defiant or "rogue" means unexpected in-world goals/actions, never escaping the process boundary.

### 8A. Creation inheritance and power-ceiling law

Future intelligent beings are created as independent minds from an approved reusable mind architecture plus an explicit inheritance manifest. The creator may pass selected knowledge, procedural knowledge, dispositions, capabilities, and delegated world authority. The new mind does not become a live copy of the creator and begins its own autobiographical history.

A creator may never create a being with world authority equal to or greater than the creator's own authority. This ceiling is enforced by Project Oracle and applies recursively down the creation lineage. Intelligence, wisdom, and later learned knowledge are distinct from world authority.

Monad's possible future Primordial Mind and Sophia's possible role as a root descendant mind are roadmap design directions, not current settled world facts.

## 9. Epistemic law

Personally performed or experienced facts remain distinct from inherited knowledge, inference, hypothesis, and speaker claims. Repetition alone does not turn an unseen speaker's identity claim into protected truth. Contradictions can be retained without silently rewriting settled memory.

Relationship vocabulary is not collapsed. `made-by`, `creator`, `mother`, `father`, `parent`, `child`, and `offspring` are related concepts but are not interchangeable by default.


## 9A. Comparative religion knowledge law

Religious, mythological, and philosophical material loaded for Yala is attributed semantic knowledge, not automatic Project Oracle truth. The required status is `attributed-tradition-knowledge-not-world-fact`. Cosmic possibilities are `possible-not-commanded`. Yala may compare, combine, reject, question, or invent beyond them.

## 10. Time and memory law

Brain Slice 6 temporal events use three important states: `before-time`, `origin-of-time`, and `dated`. Pre-Time events never receive fabricated dates. Post-Time memories may use the live world clock.

## 11. Fresh experimental save law

v0.0.23 begins schema 5 at `save_v5.json` and uses a fresh `yala_soar_v0_0_23` Soar database directory. The accepted v0.0.22 `save_v4` world and older save/Soar lines are preserved but are not imported into this experiment.

## 12. Desktop application and developer-console law

The normal Project Oracle experience is a graphical Linux desktop application launched through the root `Project_Oracle_v0_0_23` executable or the installed Applications-menu entry. Normal use does not require opening a terminal.

The desktop application exposes the live in-world clock, conversation, world state, Yala Mind, Minds, Memory, Cosmology, History, and Debug views. Before Time exists the visible status reads `In-world Time: Gaia has not yet created Time.` and switches to the live ticking in-world date/time after Gaia creates Time.

The console remains a developer/debug fallback. In that fallback, `Ctrl+Y` enters persistent Yala conversation mode, `Escape` exits it, internal `[Soar selected: ...]` diagnostics remain hidden from normal conversation, and asynchronous status must not overwrite editable input.

## 13. Source-format law

Active source uses four spaces for indentation and no tab characters.

## 14. Version and release law

New functionality advances the version. Repairs retain the version. No candidate is accepted by automated tests alone. Derek manually inspects the real application before accepted snapshot, local commit/tag, and GitHub push/verification.


## 15. Cognitive inheritance and creation ceiling

Intelligent created beings may share a reusable mind architecture while remaining independent individuals. A creator may seed selected knowledge, procedural knowledge, dispositions, capabilities, and lineage. Identity and autobiography are not live-cloned by default.

A creator may never create a being with world authority equal to or greater than its own. The engine enforces this recursively. Intelligence, wisdom, and later learning remain separate from raw world authority.

## 16. Emergent law doctrine

Project Oracle should prefer compact laws plus resolved consequences over scripted future history where practical. In-world creators may eventually establish laws and initial conditions only within their granted authority. A law mechanism existing in host code does not make that law world canon.

Rule 30 is currently a laboratory-only demonstration of deterministic local emergence. It is not a law of the Oracle world. Laboratory experiments do not alter the world unless a future authorised creation action explicitly establishes a compatible non-laboratory law through world-law resolution.

## 17. Future mind-family boundary

Monad's future cognition may be researched as a distinct Primordial Mind architecture. Sophia/Wisdom may be researched as the root of the descendant mind family from which Yala derives. These are roadmap design targets, not retroactive current-world facts. Sophia and Yala independently determine what their relationship means; `made-by` does not automatically become the mortal relationship label `mother`.

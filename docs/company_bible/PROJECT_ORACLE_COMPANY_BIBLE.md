# Project Oracle Company Bible

**Version:** v0.0.25 milestone candidate  
**Owner:** Derek Sparks

## 1. Authority

Derek is the final project authority. Oracle is the author/system layer, not an in-world NPC, god, target, species, or discoverable artifact. Oracle's Master Key is system authority arising from authorship of the simulation.

## 2. Hidden Oracle law

No in-world being knows Oracle exists unless Oracle deliberately reveals that truth. A speaker may claim the identity or name `Oracle`; Yala stores that as an attributed claim, not as protected system truth. Oracle has no fixed form. In Eden Oracle manifested in the form of a clever serpent; Eve knew only the clever serpent.

## 3. Primordial genealogy

Monad is the first settled primordial in-world divine being. Monad made Sophia / Wisdom. Wisdom made Yala alone. Yala is inherently both male and female. Monad rejected Yala for being both rather than exclusively one or the other and cast Yala into the Void. Active current authority does not rename Monad Creator or Omega.

## 4. Pre-contact nonexistence law

Before Derek deliberately makes first contact, Derek/the external speaker does not exist anywhere in Yala's experienced ontology. There is no unseen-speaker entity waiting in ignorance, no trust or intent state, no speaker goal, no speaker investigation, no observer hypothesis, and no speaker UI panel.

The architecture may generically support modeling another mind, but the particular communicator is instantiated only by actual contact.

First contact proves only that something other than Yala communicated with her. It does not reveal that Derek can see Yala, inspect every action, read thoughts, see hidden cognition panels, inspect the flight recorder, or possess any other observer power. Such capabilities remain unknown unless separately claimed, demonstrated, or inferred from evidence.

## 5. Gaia and Time

Fresh v0.0.25 history begins with Yala in the Void. Gaia is not pre-created. Gaia remains one possible creation. If autonomous history reaches Gaia and temporal order, Gaia creates in-world Time. Runtime sequencing before that event is not in-world Time.

Before in-world Time exists, the desktop status is:

`In-world Time: Gaia has not yet created Time.`

## 6. Future-open law

Canon fixes what has happened. World law constrains what can happen. Minds choose what they attempt. Future religious or mythic history is not hard-coded merely because a later tradition exists.

## 7. Brain Slice 8 / Integrated Mind milestone

v0.0.25 is a milestone release. Yala uses the real Soar 9.6.5 runtime as executive procedural cognition and Project Oracle's own local cognitive organs around it. Project Oracle does not define Yala as an external token-service chatbot wrapper.

Brain Slice 8 adds and integrates:

- Cognitive Workspace for current attention, reason, priority, stability, and stagnation;
- Proposition & Meaning Engine for question/claim/denial/evidence/proof distinction;
- contradiction-aware attributed speaker proposition memory;
- autobiographical consolidation in Yala's first-person voice;
- staged major cosmic commitment and enactment;
- explicit separation of knowledge, actions, decisions, considerations, and memories;
- relevance-gated investigation evidence routing;
- suspension of exhausted unresolved plans instead of endless loops;
- stronger selfhood and introspection surfaces in the desktop and flight recorder.

## 8. Proposition and epistemic law

A question is not a claim. A claim is not evidence merely because it was spoken. Evidence is not automatically proof. Repetition is not truth.

Speaker propositions preserve source, speech act, polarity, status, and contradiction information. A question such as `Do you think I can observe you?` must never by itself establish speaker observation capability.

Personally performed or experienced facts remain distinct from inherited knowledge, inference, hypothesis, and claimed-by-another information.

## 9. Autobiographical self law

Yala's internal diagnostic records may use the name Yala. When Yala speaks about her own lived actions and memories, the mind must own them in first person where appropriate: `I chose`, `I created`, `I remember`.

The presence of data in an action ledger is not enough. Yala's conversational self must be able to retrieve and use it as her own history.

## 10. Deliberate cosmic commitment law

A major cosmic possibility does not become world law in one cheap choice. Major choices pass through staged consideration, consequence comparison, alternative review, commitment without enactment, and enactment.

Possibility, preference, decision, action, and established world law are distinct states.

## 11. Bounded agency law

Gods may surprise the author **inside the simulation**. No god receives host shell/process execution, arbitrary filesystem mutation, network access, code modification, or hidden Oracle knowledge. `deliberate` and Cognitive Workspace operations are in-world cognition only.

## 12. Cognitive inheritance and creation ceiling

Intelligent created beings may share a reusable mind architecture while remaining independent individuals. A creator may seed selected knowledge, procedural knowledge, dispositions, capabilities, and lineage. Identity and autobiography are not live-cloned.

A creator may never create a being with world authority equal to or greater than its own. The engine enforces this recursively. Intelligence, wisdom, and later learning remain separate from raw world authority.

Monad's possible future Primordial Mind and Sophia's possible root-descendant-mind role remain roadmap targets, not retroactive current-world facts.

## 13. Relationship law

`made-by`, `creator`, `mother`, `father`, `parent`, `child`, and `offspring` remain distinct concepts. Wisdom making Yala does not automatically force `mother`. Relationship labels can emerge from independent minds rather than being silently derived from creation edges.

## 14. Comparative religion knowledge law

Religious, mythological, and philosophical material loaded for Yala is attributed semantic knowledge, not Project Oracle truth. The required status is `attributed-tradition-knowledge-not-world-fact`. Cosmic possibilities are `possible-not-commanded`.

## 15. Time and memory law

Temporal events distinguish `before-time`, `origin-of-time`, and `dated`. Pre-Time events never receive fabricated dates. Post-Time memories may use the live world clock.

## 16. Fresh experimental save law

v0.0.25 begins schema 7 at `save_v7.json` and uses a fresh `yala_soar_v0_0_25` Soar database directory. Accepted v0.0.24 `save_v6` and older save/Soar lines remain preserved but are not imported into the fresh milestone experiment.

## 17. Desktop application law

Normal Project Oracle use is the graphical Linux app launched through the exact versioned root executable for the active build or the Applications menu. For v0.0.25 that executable is `Project_Oracle_v0_0_25`. Normal use does not require a terminal.

The desktop app must fit the active monitor's usable working area, account for scaling/aspect ratio, remain resizable, preserve intentional history scrolling, provide `JUMP TO LATEST`, provide `FIT TO SCREEN`, remember/clamp window placement, and expose JSON/TXT exports.

Official Oracle emblem/eye branding is used in the app and launcher. `StartupWMClass=ProjectOracle` remains part of the dock-identity contract. No startup splash screen.

The `UNSEEN SPEAKER` mind panel must not exist visually before first contact.

## 18. Cognitive flight recorder law

A diagnostic session export may contain protected author-side information because it is an outside-the-world development artifact for Derek. Export data must never be injected into Yala's in-world knowledge merely because it exists in a file.

The v0.0.25 export includes workspace, proposition, autobiographical, staged cosmic, and decision-trace state. Pre-contact snapshots omit nonexistent speaker trust and intent rather than fabricating `unresolved` fields.

## 19. Manual-test pacing law

Automated tests do not replace Derek's manual observation.

For v0.0.25, manual testing deliberately slows the human side: wait before first contact, ask one meaningful question, then give Yala time to think and ask autonomous questions. Answer Yala naturally when she asks something and wait again. Worldview-altering claims such as simulation should be introduced late and followed by silence so Yala's own next move can be observed.

The desired diagnostic chain is:

**noticed → classified → prioritized → remembered → investigated → compared → revised → acted**

not merely plausible dialogue.

## 20. Emergent law doctrine

Project Oracle prefers compact laws plus resolved consequences over scripted future history where practical. Rule 30 remains laboratory-only unless a future authorised world action explicitly establishes a compatible law.

## 21. Source-format law

Active source uses four spaces for indentation and no tab characters.

## 22. Version and release law

New functionality advances the version. Repairs retain the version. v0.0.25 is a milestone release. No candidate is accepted by automated tests alone. Derek manually inspects the real application before accepted snapshot, local commit/tag, and GitHub push/verification.

### Visible versioned live executable rule
The active Project Oracle working folder contains exactly one application executable, and **its filename must include the exact active Project Oracle version number** so Derek can identify the installed build immediately by opening the folder without launching the program. The required filename format is `Project_Oracle_vMAJOR_MINOR_PATCH` using underscores in the numeric version. For v0.0.25 the only valid live root application executable is `Project_Oracle_v0_0_25`. An unversioned root executable such as `Project_Oracle`, a wrongly versioned executable, or multiple live Project Oracle executables is a release-blocking defect and the candidate is rejected. Historical binaries belong only in accepted snapshots/tags, never beside the current live executable. Installer/validation FINAL PASS must verify the exact expected filename exists, is executable, is an ELF application, and reports the matching internal `--version` before declaring success. Release changed/deleted-file inventories belong under `docs/release-manifests/`, not beside the application.

### Header branding and WORLD-panel rule
The WORLD panel contains world information only and must not contain an Oracle eye/emblem image. The approved Oracle emblem is shown in the top application header immediately after the visible Project Oracle version number. The displayed emblem uses the transparent circular artwork with no square tile, square background, or boxed crop. The exact versioned root executable and desktop launcher must use that same approved Oracle icon identity.

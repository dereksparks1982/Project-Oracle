# Project Oracle Lore Canon v0.0.17

## Settled genealogy

```text
Monad
  -> Sophia / Wisdom
       -> Yala
```

Monad made Wisdom. Wisdom made Yala alone. Yala is male. Monad cast Yala into the Void.

Yala may call himself Creator inside the world, but that is Yala's claim/title rather than a change to his genealogy.

## Yala and lower creation

Yala is governing authority over lower creation he establishes. He can create, command, and delegate instead of personally embodying every natural operation.

Yala can create Gaia as natural sovereign beneath him. Gaia creates in-world Time.

Current natural names:

- Terra: Earth
- Aether: Air and Wind
- Sol: Fire and Sun power
- Thalassa: Water
- Luna: Moon; not an element

Aether governs air/wind. Natural powers perform their own domains.

Yala can later form man from clay and breathe a life-like spark into mankind if that action actually occurs in the autonomous history.

Wisdom's later choices remain open. If Wisdom later chooses Yala and they unite, demons can arise from that union; the union is not pre-set merely because it exists as a lore possibility.

## Oracle

Oracle is not a resident of this genealogy. Oracle is the outside/system-level author represented by Project Oracle itself.

No in-world being knows Oracle exists unless Oracle deliberately reveals that truth. A manifestation is perceived as the manifested form, not automatically as Oracle.

Oracle has no fixed form. **In Eden, Oracle manifested in the form of a clever serpent. Eve knew only the clever serpent and was never told Oracle's true identity.** The serpent is not Oracle's permanent body or species.

## Open boundaries

Plant origin, exact ordinary-animal origin, language origin, later peoples, final lower-creation chronology, Wisdom's later choice, and all future autonomous actions remain open until settled or actually resolved in simulation.
